# Source images — drop originals here

**This is the folder to put your raw image files in.**

Put them here exactly as they come from wherever you generated or shot them —
full size, backgrounds still on, no need to rename or resize anything. I take it
from there: background removal, cropping, resizing, and converting to the
formats the site needs.

## Why this folder and not `public/images`

Anything inside `public/` is served to the public — anyone could type the
address and download it. These originals are large, unprocessed, and not meant
to be the files visitors receive, so they live outside it. The finished,
web-ready versions end up in `public/images/`.

## What makes a usable image

- **As large as you have.** Around 2000px on the long edge is ideal. I can shrink
  an image well; I cannot invent detail that was never there.
- **PNG or JPG** both fine. PNG is better if it already has a transparent
  background.
- **A plain, even background** is what makes the cutout clean.

## On background removal

Send them with backgrounds still on. I'll do the cutout and show you the result
at full size so you can judge the edges yourself.

It usually works well on a plain background. It tends to struggle with motion
blur, loose or flyaway hair, and backgrounds close in tone to the subject — on a
dark hero those failures show up as a pale halo around the figure. If that
happens I'll say so rather than quietly shipping it, and you can run that one
image through a dedicated background-removal tool instead.

## Photographs of children

If any image is a real photograph of a real child, the academy needs the
guardian's recorded consent before it goes on a public page. AI-generated or
stock figures carry no such requirement. Tell me which category an image falls
into if it isn't obvious.
